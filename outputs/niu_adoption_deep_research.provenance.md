# Provenance: NIU Adoption Deep Research

Date: 2026-05-01

## Research Question

What would make USFMAE-IS useful and adoptable by Northern Illinois University, based on verified online information?

## Method

Used current online sources, prioritizing official NIU pages, U.S. Department of Education / Federal Student Aid guidance, ADA.gov, and W3C accessibility standards. The analysis distinguishes between what NIU already provides through MyNIU/PeopleSoft and where USFMAE-IS could add value.

## Key Source Claims Used

- NIU MyNIU is the hub for student-system access, financial aid, delegate access, shared access, and financial management systems.
- NIU Financial Account Tile includes account balance, payments, charges due, payment history, pending aid, term account detail, statements, 1098-T, and student permissions.
- NIU tells students to check MyNIU To-Do List / Tasks Tile for financial aid verification and required documents.
- NIU states holds can block registration and directs students to MyNIU for hold information.
- NIU PeopleSoft governance controls enterprise administrative system changes and considers cost, maintenance, and benefit.
- NIU procurement includes accessibility review and requires VPAT WCAG / ACR information.
- NIU information security has vendor/app assessment processes and policies applying to third-party support contractors.
- FERPA guidance limits education-record access to school officials with legitimate educational interests.
- FERPA guidance defines PII broadly and includes direct and indirect identifiers.
- FSA guidance says Title IV institutions must protect student financial aid information under GLBA-related obligations.
- DOJ Title II web rule uses WCAG 2.1 Level AA as the technical standard for state/local government web and mobile app accessibility.
